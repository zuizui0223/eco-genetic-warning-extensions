# Phase O — high-precision validation of the Phase-D narrow-R4 frontier

Phase L showed that the historical Phase-D neighbour R3 labels do not by themselves identify biological block heterogeneity. Phase O precision-expands the exact Phase-D design.

## Fixed design

- master seeds `20290310–20290314` only;
- `p_star = 0.325, 0.350, 0.375` only;
- independent source reconstruction for each `p_star`, exactly as Phase D;
- same `kappa_mu=0.35`, Aref=1, interaction kappa=4.5, 30+90 deterioration and barrier increase 0.30;
- 100 attempted replicates per master seed and `p_star`;
- historical `[0.30,0.70]` five-block gate unchanged;
- no warning/diversity outcomes used.

## Prefix provenance gate

All 15 seed×`p_star` first-20 eligible/loss prefixes must exactly reproduce the locked Phase-D counts. Any mismatch blocks interpretation.

## Primary decision

Apply the historical classifier to the five 100-attempt blocks at each `p_star`.

- central `.350` remains R4 and at least one immediate neighbour remains outside R4 → a bounded high-precision frontier difference remains;
- all three are R4 → withdraw the old claim that immediate-neighbour R3 labels establish a narrow R4 region;
- central `.350` itself leaves R4 → the original recovered anchor is not stable at the new precision and the frontier claim must be restructured around the full high-precision map.

Equal-rate and finite-sample diagnostics are reported for every `p_star`.

## Stop rule

No replacement seeds, new `p_star` values, finer frontier search, R4-band changes or further precision increase merely to preserve the historical narrow-R4 statement.
