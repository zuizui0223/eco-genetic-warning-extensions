# Phase V result — fresh fixed-domain genetic-warning replication

Phase V prospectively tested whether the parent H2-R baseline-relative genetic-warning result replicates in one independent fresh-seed ensemble without recalibration or endpoint selection.

## Decision

**`strict_replication`**

The preregistered event-pair criterion was met at all six endpoints. A later full-denominator audit supersedes its predictive interpretation but does not alter this historical protocol fact.

## Post-review interpretation correction

All six thresholds also fired in all 49 non-event trajectories in this ensemble; in the inherited ensemble they fired in all 48 non-events. Fixed generation-30 binary-marker AUC was near chance. By the full horizon every marker was a constant positive; specificity was therefore zero and AUC was 0.5. The allowed current claim is replicated **event-conditional ordering without event discrimination**, not validated predictive early warning. A later separately preregistered exploratory continuous audit found no portable coordinate/time signal; see `docs/PREPUBLICATION_REVIEW_AUDIT.md`.

## Warning ordering

For each of `H_alpha` and `H_gamma` at 5%, 10% and 20% baseline-relative decline:

- valid same-trajectory warning/loss pairs: **33**;
- warning leads: **33**;
- ties: **0**;
- lags: **0**;
- lead fraction: **1.0**;
- exact one-sided binomial `p` versus lead probability 0.5: **1.1641532182693481e-10**.

The five fresh master-seed blocks contributed `7, 7, 7, 7, 5` valid pairs at every endpoint, and every valid pair was a lead.

## Scientific correction

The original parent benchmark contained 35/35 leads at all six relative endpoints. Phase V independently recovered the same ordering pattern as 33/33 leads at all six endpoints in fresh unused seeds.

The original protocol-level claim was strengthened from:

> baseline-relative genetic erosion can precede functional loss in one finite calibrated benchmark

to:

> **within the frozen symmetric H2-R domain, baseline-relative `H_alpha` and `H_gamma` erosion at 5%, 10% and 20% strictly replicated as leading realised functional-trait loss across two independent seed ensembles.**

This remains a historical statement about event-conditional ordering. The post-review full-denominator audit withdraws the predictive-validity reading.

## Why this matters for the natural-state programme

The replication makes the condition-first interpretation positive rather than merely restrictive. Once the loss-generating eco-genetic domain is held fixed, the warning ordering is reproducible across an independent seed ensemble. The unresolved issue is therefore not whether warning can ever replicate, but **which measured ecological state defines the domain within which a warning claim is transportable**.

That is the direct bridge to the natural empirical programme: urban, island and other fragmented systems must first be assigned to a measured loss-generating state from interaction, function, process-specific connectivity, cohort-specific genetics and ecological memory before a genetic warning is expected to transfer.

## Boundaries retained

Phase V does not establish:

- a universal absolute or relative genetic-warning threshold;
- portability to different eco-genetic domains;
- an isolated effect of recurrent-transition direction;
- warning validity under every fragmentation, movement or interaction-network closure.

The prior absolute-threshold negative result is unchanged. Historical Protocol 003 remains bounded portability evidence across non-matched domains.
