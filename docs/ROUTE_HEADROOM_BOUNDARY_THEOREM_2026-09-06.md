# Exact route-headroom boundary for the finite fragmentation closure

Status: **exact one-step state-space boundary plus an exact matched-marginal coverage–reserve certificate**. This result identifies the next interaction-side branch condition in the declared finite closure. It is not a theorem for the eventual all-patch realised-trait endpoint in natural systems.

## 1. Exact local branch surface

Under the locked full-feedback interaction update,

\[
q_j^+=\sigma\{\kappa(a_j d_j S_j-\theta_t)\},
\]

where

\[
S_j=0.6q_j+0.3T_j+0.1G_j,
\]

`a_j=A_j/A_ref`, `d_j=min(1,N_j/K_j)`, and `kappa=4.5`.

For a target interaction state `c`, define

\[
\boxed{
H_j(t;c)=a_jd_jS_j-\theta_t-\frac{\operatorname{logit}(c)}{\kappa}.
}
\]

Because the logistic map is strictly increasing,

\[
\boxed{
H_j(t;c)\ge0
\iff
q_j(t+1)\ge c.
}
\]

For the shared high-trait/high-allele switch `c=0.625`,

\[
\frac{\operatorname{logit}(0.625)}{4.5}=0.1135168053,
\]

so in the locked equal-area setting

\[
\boxed{
H_j(t)=d_j[0.6q_j+0.3T_j+0.1G_j]-\theta_t-0.1135168053.
}
\]

This is the exact local **route-headroom** coordinate. Positive headroom means that the next interaction state remains on the `q>=0.625` side; negative headroom means it crosses below that side.

## 2. Operator decomposition of headroom

The route margin is monotone in each instantaneous support coordinate:

\[
\frac{\partial H}{\partial q}=0.6d>0,
\qquad
\frac{\partial H}{\partial T}=0.3d>0,
\qquad
\frac{\partial H}{\partial G}=0.1d>0,
\qquad
\frac{\partial H}{\partial d}=S>0
\]

for positive support and unsaturated density.

Define the normalized eco-genetic bundle

\[
B=0.75T+0.25G.
\]

Then

\[
S=0.6q+0.4B=q+0.4(B-q).
\]

Relative to the q-only support signal, direct eco-genetic feedback shifts route headroom by exactly

\[
\boxed{
H_{\rm full}-H_{q\text{-only}}
=0.4d(B-q)
}
\]

in the locked equal-area setting. Thus direct recoupling moves the local branch margin toward the eco-genetic bundle: it increases headroom where `B>q` and decreases it where `B<q`.

The other exact operators act upstream of later headroom rather than as simultaneous additive terms: q-dependent allele selection changes future `G`, allele-linked recruitment contracts the pre-selection trait–allele mismatch that feeds future `T`, and density feedback changes future `d`. The headroom surface therefore provides the common state-space coordinate through which those distinct operators later affect the next interaction branch.

## 3. Exact initial matched-marginal certificate

For the original four-patch full-feedback construction,

\[
q=(0.65,0.75,0.85,0.95),
\]

with the common bundle values `(0.20,0.40,0.60,0.80)` either aligned (`AA`) or reversed (`RR`). Initial population equals carrying capacity, so `d=1`. At the generation-1 barrier,

\[
\theta_1=0.5025,
\qquad
\theta_1+0.1135168053=0.6160168053.
\]

The support and route-headroom fields are therefore:

| condition | support S | headroom H | H>=0 patches | max H | min H |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| AA | `.47,.61,.75,.89` | `-.1460,-.0060,.1340,.2740` | **2/4** | **0.27398** | -0.14602 |
| RR | `.71,.69,.67,.65` | `.0940,.0740,.0540,.0340` | **4/4** | **0.09398** | +0.03398 |

Both conditions have exactly the same mean support `0.68`, hence the same mean headroom `0.0639832`. But their headroom variances differ 49-fold, exactly as their support variances do.

This yields a precise **coverage–reserve trade-off** in the opening state:

- `RR` has broader immediate coverage: all four patches remain above the next-step switch, but the deepest reserve is only `0.09398`;
- `AA` sacrifices the two weakest patches immediately, but concentrates much more reserve into the strongest patch (`0.27398`).

Thus the matched-marginal construction does not merely produce different endpoints. It redistributes the same mean route headroom between **number of currently safe patches** and **depth of the strongest local reserve**.

## 4. Why this clarifies the earlier full-feedback result

The prospective full-feedback experiment did not support a universal directional `AA` advantage. The headroom certificate explains why that was never expected to be a one-dimensional ordering.

The aligned construction concentrates support and reserve, while the reversed construction equalizes support near the branch boundary. Direct eco-genetic feedback therefore creates a short-horizon coverage benefit for the reversed state even though q-dependent allele sorting can later favour compatible local cores. Recruitment and direct recoupling can repair mismatch; rising forcing and density feedback consume headroom.

The long-horizon fate is consequently generated by the temporal competition among operators acting on a common route margin, not by the sign of one alignment score.

## 5. Claim ceiling

`H=0` is an exact boundary for the **next interaction state relative to q=0.625** in the declared closure. It is not, by itself, an exact predictor of the eventual all-patch realised-high-trait loss endpoint because realised trait state can persist after potential viability changes and because recruitment, selection, demographic dynamics and finite stochasticity intervene over later generations.

The opening AA/RR coverage–reserve numbers are exact for the declared four-patch construction and generation-1 forcing. They are not universal natural thresholds or evidence that spatial concentration is generally protective.
