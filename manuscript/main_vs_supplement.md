# Integrated manuscript allocation

## Main text

The Letter uses exactly six main display items and no main-text tables or text boxes. Main text retains only the causal steps, calibration decisions, and uncertainty summaries required to evaluate the paper's central warning-portability argument.

### Figure 1 — eco-genetic causal architecture
Conceptual synthesis: fragmentation, interaction state, realised high-trait state, local effective size, genetic diversity, warning, and functional-trait loss.

### Figure 2 — source feasibility across recurrent-transition coordinates
Common-grid Stage I source reconstruction and projection. Supplementary tables retain the cell-level data underlying Figure 2.

### Figure 3 — functional-loss regimes plus complete-candidate composition
Common-grid warning-blind Stage II result, including rapid-loss, seed-heterogeneous, and persistence candidate composition. The former stand-alone three-number composition display is absorbed here.

### Figure 4 — cumulative warning and functional-loss incidence
Retains baseline-eligible completed trajectories and plots cumulative observed incidence across each calibrated horizon. It is descriptive rather than a classical competing-risk estimator because warning and loss can both occur on one trajectory.

### Figure 5 — full attempted denominator, censoring, and ordering
Shows all 100 attempted trajectories per endpoint, including source failure, baseline ineligibility, censoring, lead, tie, and lag. Supplementary endpoint tables underlying Figures 4–6 retain exact counts; these are reported as Table S4 and its machine-readable companion files. Table S5 records calibrated-domain schedule and timing-normalization details.

### Figure 6 — absolute and horizon-normalized positive lead time
Secondary conditional diagnostic among observed leading pairs. Conventional medians and whole-trajectory bootstrap intervals are shown in generations and as fractions of each calibrated horizon; the text explicitly conditions interpretation on Figure 4 event incidence and Figure 5 availability.

## Supplementary Material

Supplement includes full mathematical results and proofs, life-cycle specification, migration bounds, source/projection invariants, complete Stage I–III parameter and seed bookkeeping, Protocol 002 and Protocol 003 calibration rules and amendments, inherited H3 effect-size audit, the post-review Stage III timing/uncertainty audit, full endpoint/censoring tables, and provenance hashes.

### Supplementary Figure S1 — fixed-area fragmentation gradient
New post-review Type S sensitivity using fresh seeds and the same mutation-primary H1/H3 closure. It shows the paired response across 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, and 16 isolated equal patches and retains the non-monotonic realised high-trait-mass response.
