## Phase J headline and gate correction

Four prospectively fixed five-seed panels at the same fixed `m=.10` condition were **R4 / R3 / R4 / R4**. Across all 20 fresh blocks:

- 19/20 inside `[.30,.70]`;
- 1/20 above (`.75`);
- total eligible/loss = 342/180;
- pooled loss = `.5263`;
- unweighted mean block loss = `.5247`;
- median = `.5132`;
- block-rate sample SD = `.1182`.

For the observed 20 rates there are `C(20,5)=15,504` possible five-block panels:

- 11,628 = **75%** finite R4 certificates;
- 3,876 = **25%** finite R3 certificates.

Exact design identity: if an independent block passes an operational band with probability `q`, an all-pass `B`-block certificate passes with probability `q^B`. Therefore the historical binary R4 certificate is panel-size dependent whenever `0<q<1`.

## Evidence rules

- Parent and extension trajectories are never pooled.
- Historical Protocol 002 15/15 `no_domain_selected` remains immutable for its candidate family.
- Later finite R4 recovery does not establish warning success or a sample-size-invariant biological phase.
- Phase E `migration_rate` is allele-frequency mixing only; its exact `m=.10` R3 label is not portable across independent ensembles after Phase I/J.
- Phase F `interaction kappa` is aggregate feedback, not network simplification; Phase F is closed.
- Phase G does not show partner loss universally raises mean risk or that contribution concentration determines robustness.
- Phase H does not show rewiring is universally ineffective; network recovery is not equivalent to functional-loss distribution recovery.
- Phase I's `g↔m/2` equivalence is restricted to its declared regional paternal-gene-flow closure.
- Phase J proves the historical all-block R4 certificate is ensemble/panel dependent at the tested condition; do not add or regroup seeds to erase that result.
- Primary upstream estimands are central loss incidence and among-block heterogeneity/tails; a binary certificate is secondary and sampling-design explicit.
- Protocol 003 domains remain non-matched; their contrast is bounded portability, not a direction-only effect.
- Endpoint records sharing trajectories are correlated; uncertainty resamples whole trajectories.
- `p_star` is an effective recurrent-transition equilibrium, not an empirical mutation rate.
