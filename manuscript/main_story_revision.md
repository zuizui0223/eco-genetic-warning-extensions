# Main-story revision after full-denominator audit

## Two load-bearing claims

### 1. State sufficiency requires joint representation

The constructive aligned/anti-aligned pair is the central model result. Common census, interaction, allele-frequency and trait marginals, `H_alpha`, `H_gamma` and `F_ST` can be identical while patchwise cross-layer alignment changes the exact next transition by `0.2543`. The fixed long-horizon comparison does not establish a directional loss-risk effect and remains explicitly bounded.

### 2. Empirical state claims require measurement and representation gates

Natural analyses follow one sequence:

`candidate-state adequacy -> information-preserving representation -> residual origin/history test`.

Honshu–Izu and Zurich illustrate residual-context redundancy after partial states; *Oenothera* illustrates a missing mating-connectivity coordinate; *Eschscholzia* illustrates an inadequate availability proxy plus a primary metadata non-identifiability; *Campanula* illustrates loss of mechanistic distinction through preprocessing.

A direct urban-versus-island convergence test is **not identifiable from the current Honshu–Izu and Zurich archives**. Their process-state coordinates and realised-function responses are not semantically harmonized, and `origin` is perfectly confounded with study/protocol identity because there is only one archive per origin. This is a design boundary, not evidence against convergence.

The stronger cross-origin hypothesis is therefore retained prospectively only under three additional requirements: the same biological state coordinates, the same direct functional response, and independent replication of origin apart from study identity. The minimum identifiable design is at least two independently measured systems per origin or one coordinated multi-origin protocol, with whole-system transfer validation.

## Supporting boundaries

- The symmetric warning benchmarks retain 35/35 and 33/33 event-conditional leads, but all six thresholds also fired in 48/48 and 49/49 non-events. They are a validation-failure example, not a positive predictive headline.
- Recurrent-turnover calibration, connectivity portability and partner perturbations delimit the tested model domain.
- Phase U/N/T results are precision-bounded nulls, not equivalence results.
- Access STOPs remain provenance/non-identifiability boundaries, not ecological findings.
- Urban–island convergence remains open; the current contribution is an explicit identifiability gate rather than a pooled origin effect.
