# Hypothesis-by-condition recovery ledger

This ledger records **recovered result → condition → remaining boundary**. Protocol chronology is secondary. After Phase J, R1/R2/R3/R4 are finite-panel calibration labels; the primary biological object is the distribution of functional-loss events.

## C0 — an interaction-dependent high-function state can exist

**Recovered.** The pinned parent interaction map permits a high-function state under explicit gain/barrier conditions, and finite preparation recovers high-function sources in a bounded region.

**Boundary.** The analytical `K>4` certificate is model-specific, not a universal ecological threshold.

## C1 — fragmentation can become interaction-mediated functional fragmentation

**Recovered.** Among 1,055 H1-qualified parent pairs, equal isolation at fixed total area reduced final interaction, local effective size and realised high-trait mass relative to the matched one-large state. A fresh 1–16 patch sensitivity recovered 1,037 prepared sources and showed the major change after the first split.

**Boundary.** `Functional fragmentation` already has a landscape-connectivity meaning. Here **interaction-mediated functional fragmentation** denotes loss/destabilisation of the interaction support required for realised ecological function. Spatial fragmentation is not equated with interaction-network simplification.

## C2 — the functional-loss distribution is conditioned by eco-genetic closure

### C2a — recurrent turnover changes source feasibility and loss outcomes

Across the original 15 coordinates, **2,269/3,375** source attempts supported preparation/projection. Among 648 complete common-grid panels, 322 were rapid-loss, 242 persistence and 84 seed-heterogeneous; none passed the original strict R4 rule. The historical **15/15 `no_domain_selected`** result remains immutable for that candidate family.

Prospective warning-blind high-rep work later produced finite R4 certificates twice at `kappa_mu=.35, p_star=.35`; immediate tested neighbours were R3 in those panels.

**Post-Phase-J interpretation.** Finite intermediate-risk panels exist, but the binary R4 certificate is not itself a sample-size-invariant biological regime. The recurrent-turnover result is retained as source-feasibility and loss-distribution evidence, plus a bounded finite-panel certificate recovery.

### C2b — genetic-state connectivity reshuffles stochastic loss histories

Phase E paired the same prepared sources across allele-frequency mixing levels:

| `m` | pooled loss | locked finite label |
|---:|---:|---|
| 0 | .571 | R4 |
| .025 | .549 | R4 |
| .05 | .593 | R4 |
| .10 | .626 | R3 |
| .20 | .604 | R3 |

Paired status switched in both directions. This bidirectional trajectory effect is the robust result.

**Boundary.** Phase I independently classified the same nominal `m=.10` condition R4, and Phase J demonstrated ensemble sensitivity at fixed `m=.10`. Therefore the Phase-E `m=.10` R3 label is a locked finite observation, **not a portable categorical connectivity boundary**. `migration_rate` remains allele-frequency mixing, not demographic, pollen, seed, pollinator or recolonisation movement.

### C2c — aggregate interaction support separates source establishment from later loss

**Recovered bounded negative result.** Phase F tested `interaction kappa = 3.0, 4.5, 6.0` prospectively.

| `kappa` | eligible | pooled loss | finite label |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 3.0 | 77/100 | .468 | R4 |
| 4.5 | 94/100 | .521 | R4 |
| 6.0 | 87/100 | .552 | R4 |

All 15 tested seed-block rates were inside `[.30,.70]`; source eligibility nevertheless differed.

**Boundary.** The kappa axis is closed. `interaction kappa` is aggregate positive-feedback strength, **not partner richness**, connectance, pollinator diversity or network dimensionality. No finer/wider kappa search is opened to manufacture a certificate boundary.

### C2d — partner loss separates mean risk from among-block heterogeneity

Phase G used one intact four-partner control and three predeclared one-partner-loss architectures matched for `4→3` richness change and mean retained support `.75`.

| condition | pooled loss | seed-rate range | finite label |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| intact | .544 | .129 | R4 |
| even loss | .567 | .261 | R3 |
| graded loss | .556 | .353 | R3 |
| dominant loss | .578 | .235 | R3 |

Paired status switched in both directions; secondary Cochran Q `p=.943`. Contribution concentration did not separate the three loss architectures.

**Conclusion.** Partner loss broadened stochastic/block outcomes more than it shifted mean failure incidence. After Phase J, the R4→R3 change is retained as a finite-panel observation, not a universal partner-loss phase transition.

### C2e — explicit rewiring can restore network state without restoring functional-loss dynamics

Phase H introduced six candidate partners, four active edges, two latent edges, explicit edge strengths, trait matching, capacities and one fixed rewiring rule. The opening rule succeeded: fresh intact R4; matched no-rewiring partner loss R3.

Constrained rewiring remained R3 (`not_rescued`) even though:

- active edges `3→5`;
- realised connectance `.500→.833`;
- final support multiplier `.750→.844`;
- pooled loss changed only `.430→.419`;
- only one of 86 comparable trajectories changed loss status under rewiring.

**Conclusion.** Network structural/support recovery is not sufficient evidence of recovery of the downstream functional-loss distribution.

**Boundary.** Phase H does not establish that rewiring is generally ineffective. The single prospective rule is closed without tuning.

### C2f — pollen movement is process-resolved and kernel dependent at trajectory level

Phase I defined paternal pollen contribution:

`p_off=(1-g/2)p_local+(g/2)p_pool`.

For a census-weighted regional pollen pool this is exactly legacy global allele mixing `m=g/2`. At `g=.20`, **90/90** completed regional-pollen trajectories were snapshot-exact with paired legacy `m=.10` trajectories.

Regional and ring pollen at the same `g=.20` both had broadly intermediate loss distributions and finite R4 labels, but regional→ring switched 7 trajectories loss→no-loss and 8 no-loss→loss.

**Conclusion.** The spatial pollen kernel can change stochastic loss histories without necessarily shifting central incidence or a finite certificate.

**Boundary.** The exact `g↔m/2` identity is restricted to this regional paternal-gene-flow closure; it does not make legacy `migration_rate` generic pollen dispersal.

### C2g — the all-block R4 certificate is ensemble and panel-size dependent

Phase J fixed the full `m=.10` biological condition and generated 20 new seed blocks, prospectively split into four non-overlapping five-block panels. The panels classified **R4 / R3 / R4 / R4**: `ensemble_sensitive`.

Across all 20 blocks:

- 19/20 inside `[.30,.70]`;
- one upper-tail block `.75`;
- pooled loss `180/342=.526`;
- unweighted mean block loss `.525`;
- median `.513`;
- sample SD `.118`.

All `C(20,5)=15,504` possible five-block panels partition exactly into 11,628 R4 (**75%**) and 3,876 R3 (**25%**).

The design identity is exact: for independent blocks with single-block pass probability `q`, an all-pass `B`-block certificate succeeds with probability `q^B`. For `0<q<1`, the certificate therefore changes mechanically with panel size.

**Conclusion.** The historical R4 label is a **finite-panel calibration certificate**, not a sample-size-invariant biological regime. Primary biological estimands are central functional-loss incidence plus among-block heterogeneity/tail behaviour.

**Status.** Phase J closed. No seed addition, regrouping or threshold tuning is allowed to manufacture stability.

## C3 — genetic warning is a conditional possibility downstream of the loss distribution

**Recovered proof of possibility.** In one independently calibrated symmetric benchmark, baseline-relative `H_alpha` and `H_gamma` erosion at 5%, 10% and 20% preceded all 35 observed functional losses. Fixed absolute thresholds produced both leads and lags.

**Updated condition.** The loss-generating process should first be described distributionally. A finite R4-like all-pass rule may still serve as a conservative opening rule for a particular validation dataset, but it is not the biological estimand.

**Boundary.** The matched recurrent-transition-direction effect remains unresolved; no post-result tuning opens it.

## C4 — warning portability is bounded

Historical Protocol 003 separately calibrated two finite validation domains whose warning availability/ordering differed.

**Boundary.** The domains differ in recurrent-transition, ecological and deterioration parameters. This is bounded portability evidence, not a single-factor or isolated direction effect. Phase J further shows that portability of a binary upstream calibration label cannot be assumed across independent ensembles.

## Cross-system application

Urban and island systems are **contrasting causal routes**, not ecological equivalents and not shown here to share one binary regime.

The prospective comparison should estimate separately:

- habitat amount/configuration and matrix quality;
- realised interaction support and partner identity;
- functional diversity, contribution evenness and rewiring;
- pollen, seed/propagule, demographic and partner movement;
- reproductive assurance / alternative functional routes;
- realised function through time;
- central functional-loss incidence plus among-population/year heterogeneity;
- genetic state through time.

The cross-system target is a **distributional state map**: whether different urban and island mechanisms converge on similar functional-state feasibility and functional-loss distributions, not whether they receive the same small-panel R4 label.

## Stop rules

- No warning/diversity-informed event-regime selection.
- No finer `p_star`, migration or kappa tuning merely to create/widen R4.
- No partner-weight, rewiring-strength, pollen-fraction, seed-panel or R4-threshold tuning to manufacture a desired categorical result.
- No reinterpretation or overwriting of a locked finite campaign because a later ensemble differs.
- No demographic meaning assigned to allele-frequency mixing.
- No network-simplification meaning assigned to interaction kappa.
- No claim that Phase G increases average failure probability or that contribution concentration determines a biological regime.
- No claim that Phase H proves rewiring is universally ineffective.
- No claim that R4 is a sample-size-invariant biological regime after Phase J.
- If a universal hypothesis or categorical boundary fails, **record the boundary as the result rather than continuing until the hypothesis appears true**.
