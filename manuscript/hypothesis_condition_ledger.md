# Hypothesis-by-condition recovery ledger

This ledger records each proposition as **recovered result → conditions → remaining boundary**. Protocol chronology is secondary.

## C0 — an interaction-dependent high-function state can exist

**Recovered.** The pinned parent canonical interaction map permits distinct low/high states under its explicit gain/barrier conditions, and finite preparation recovers high-function sources in a bounded region.

**Conditions.** Declared positive-feedback map, area/feedback/barrier geometry, finite source reconstruction and hold. The analytical `K>4` boundary is model-specific, not a universal ecological threshold.

**Remaining boundary.** How aggregate interaction support changes finite source feasibility around the recovered event-regime anchor is the active Phase-F test.

## C1 — fragmentation can become functional fragmentation

**Recovered.** Among 1,055 parent H1-qualified paired replicates, equal isolation at fixed total area reduced final interaction, local effective size and realised high-trait mass relative to one large patch. A fresh 1–16-patch sensitivity showed the major interaction/Ne change after the first split; realised high-trait mass was not universally monotone across patch number.

**Conditions.** Prepared high-function source, declared equal-patch projection, fixed total area and finite horizon.

**Boundary.** Spatial fragmentation alone is not equated with interaction-network simplification. The current simulator represents aggregate interaction feedback, not partner-richness or connectance dynamics.

## C2 — the functional-loss regime is conditioned by eco-genetic closure

### C2a recurrent state turnover

**Recovered.** Across the original 15 recurrent-transition coordinates, 2,269/3,375 source attempts supported preparation/projection. Among 648 complete common-grid deterioration candidates, 322 were rapid-loss, 242 persistence and 84 seed-heterogeneous; none met the original strict R4 rule.

Prospective warning-blind high-rep refinement subsequently recovered R4 twice at `kappa_mu=0.35, p_star=0.35`, while immediate neighbouring `p_star` values remained R3-highrep.

**Conclusion.** The immutable 15/15 coarse-grid `no_domain_selected` result was a bounded candidate-family result, not structural impossibility. R4 exists but is narrow along the tested recurrent-transition frontier.

### C2b effective genetic connectivity

**Recovered at one R4 anchor.** Holding the recovered anchor fixed and changing only allele-frequency mixing produced:

| `m` | pooled loss | regime |
|---:|---:|---|
| 0.000 | 0.571 | R4-highrep |
| 0.025 | 0.549 | R4-highrep |
| 0.050 | 0.593 | R4-highrep |
| 0.100 | 0.626 | R3-highrep |
| 0.200 | 0.604 | R3-highrep |

Paired loss-status switches occurred in both directions. Connectivity therefore changed stochastic event-regime reproducibility without acting as a universal rescue/collapse axis.

**Boundary.** `migration_rate` is allele-frequency mixing, not demographic, pollinator, pollen, seed or recolonisation movement. The migration search is closed.

### C2c aggregate interaction support — active Phase F

**Question.** Does changing the existing positive-feedback strength alter (i) source feasibility and/or (ii) the R1–R4 functional-loss regime at the independently recovered recurrent-transition anchor?

**Prospective design.** Use only the pre-existing Protocol 002 levels `interaction kappa = 3.0, 4.5, 6.0`; fix `A_ref=1.0`, `kappa_mu=0.35`, `p_star=0.35`, no allele-frequency migration, horizon 120 and normalized barrier increase 0.30. Use five fresh seed blocks × 20 replicates. Reconstruct sources independently at every kappa because feedback strength changes H1 source geometry.

**Boundary.** `interaction kappa` is aggregate interaction-support/feedback strength. It is not partner richness, connectance, pollinator diversity or network dimensionality. The kappa search will stop after these three pre-existing levels.

## C3 — genetic warning is a conditional possibility

**Recovered proof of possibility.** In one independently calibrated symmetric benchmark, baseline-relative `H_alpha` and `H_gamma` erosion at 5%, 10% and 20% preceded all 35 observed functional losses. Fixed absolute thresholds produced both leads and lags.

**Conditions.** One calibrated eco-genetic domain, relative-to-own-baseline endpoints, censoring retained, ordering defined only when both events are observed.

**Boundary.** A matched recurrent-transition-direction effect remains unresolved. The refined `p_star` frontier did not recover the adjacent confirmed R4 interval required by the predeclared opening rule, so no direction-only warning test is opened by further tuning.

## C4 — warning portability is bounded

**Recovered.** Historical Protocol 003 warning-blind recalibration produced two evaluable domains whose warning availability/ordering differed.

**Conditions.** The domains differ in recurrent-transition, ecological and deterioration parameters.

**Boundary.** This is portability evidence across calibrated eco-genetic domains, not an isolated mutation-direction effect.

## Exact theory boundaries

The authoritative Type T derivations remain in `docs/RECURRENT_TRANSITION_DIVERSITY_THEORY.md`. They establish that:

- recurrent-transition direction has no universal signed effect on heterozygosity;
- stronger local high-state support can coincide with lower heterozygosity;
- transition strength contracts among-patch allele-frequency differences independently of direction under fixed weights;
- allele-frequency homogenisation is not a theorem of functional rescue.

## Cross-system application

Urban and island systems are contrasting routes through this condition space. A field test should estimate separately:

- spatial fragmentation / local habitat support;
- realised interaction support and partner composition;
- effective genetic connectivity;
- reproductive assurance / functional redundancy;
- realised functional performance/loss;
- genetic state through time.

The common application question is **when spatial fragmentation becomes functional fragmentation**. Actual network simplification is an empirical or future explicit-network axis; it is not inferred directly from interaction `kappa`.

## Stop rules

- No warning/diversity-informed event-regime selection.
- No finer `p_star`, migration or kappa tuning merely to create/widen R4.
- No reinterpretation of the historical 15/15 coarse-grid no-domain result.
- No demographic/dispersal meaning assigned to allele-frequency mixing.
- No network-simplification meaning assigned to interaction kappa.
- If a universal hypothesis fails but its boundary is recovered, record the boundary as the result rather than continuing until the hypothesis appears true.
