# Hypothesis-by-condition recovery ledger

## Purpose

Record every major proposition in the integrated study in the same format:

1. what was actually recovered;
2. the conditions under which that recovery applies;
3. what boundary remains to be mapped.

This prevents H1/H3 from being treated as unconditional truths while H-MD hypotheses are treated as condition-dependent.

## H1 — interaction-dependent functional-state structure

### Recovered

For the declared canonical positive-feedback interaction map, a finite analytical bistability certificate exists when the feedback geometry satisfies the stated conditions; within the strict bistable interval the low and high fixed points are stable and the middle fixed point unstable. The high-trait viability margin can differ between low and high interaction branches. Finite source-preparation simulations additionally recover high-function states in a bounded subset of the declared parameter grid.

### Conditions

- specified canonical interaction map and trait-performance surface;
- feedback strength/area combination sufficiently strong for the analytical multi-state geometry;
- barrier lies in the appropriate interval;
- finite stochastic source preparation must actually reconstruct and hold the high state.

The analytical `K>4` geometry is a model-specific reference, not a universal ecological threshold and not an observed fragmentation threshold.

### Boundary still worth mapping

The finite source-feasibility boundary as ecological support (`A_ref`, interaction `kappa`, patch area) interacts with recurrent state turnover. H-MD-1 already shows that the genetic closure changes this finite source boundary.

## H3 — fragmentation disrupts the prepared high-function state

### Recovered

Conditional on an H1-prepared full source and the declared conservation-preserving projection, equal isolation at fixed total area reduced final interaction, local effective size and realised high-trait mass in all 1,055 qualified historical paired replicates.

A fresh 1–16-patch sensitivity confirmed that the large interaction/Ne effect appears already after the first split to two isolated patches. Realised high-trait mass is not monotone across all patch counts, and potential high-trait viability can disappear before realised occupancy does.

### Conditions

- source already qualifies under the H1 preparation/hold criterion;
- fixed total habitat area;
- declared equal-isolation projection and the corresponding migration closure;
- finite observation horizon.

Therefore H3 is not a statement that all fragmentation universally lowers every functional/genetic quantity monotonically.

### Boundary still worth mapping

Connectivity/migration is the main unclosed ecological axis. The strongest empirical urban/island application should therefore vary **effective connectivity**, not just patch number or distance. This is also where urban and island systems can differ most strongly.

## H2 relative-warning benchmark — genetic erosion can precede functional loss

### Recovered

Within one warning-blind calibrated symmetric deterioration domain, relative `H_alpha` and `H_gamma` erosion at 5%, 10% and 20% preceded all 35 observed post-baseline realised functional-trait losses. The predeclared fixed absolute thresholds produced both leads and lags in the same stored trajectories.

### Conditions

- one selected symmetric eco-genetic domain;
- warning-blind trait-loss-only calibration;
- relative-to-own-baseline endpoints;
- only trajectories with both warning and functional loss contribute to ordering;
- non-events remain censored rather than counted as warning failures.

### Boundary still worth mapping

The central question is not another threshold. It is which eco-genetic event regimes generate enough reproducible functional loss for this ordering question to be estimable at all.

## H-MD-1 — recurrent transition changes high-function source feasibility

### Recovered

Across the 15-coordinate common recurrent-transition grid, source preparation/projection support ranged from 44.89% to 86.67% (2,269/3,375 overall). Within fixed `kappa_mu` rows, support generally increased with `p_star`.

### Conditions

- independently reconstructed source at each recurrent-transition coordinate;
- declared source grid over `A_ref`, interaction `kappa`, seeds and replicates;
- exact parent life-cycle closure pinned to the canonical commit.

### Boundary still worth mapping

Joint source-feasibility surfaces over ecological support, connectivity and recurrent transition. This corresponds naturally to colonization/establishment filtering on islands and to patch occupancy under urban fragmentation.

## H-MD-2 — recurrent transition changes the functional-loss regime

### Recovered

The locked Stage II grid partitioned 648 complete candidates into 322 rapid-loss, 242 persistence and 84 seed-heterogeneous regimes before warning values were available.

Warning-blind matched reanalysis further shows, within complete candidate pairs:

- increasing `p_star` never increased pooled trait-loss rate;
- increasing `kappa_mu` never increased pooled trait-loss rate;
- the largest matched shifts were `p_star 0.25→0.50` and `kappa_mu 0.05→0.20`;
- increasing horizon from 120→240 could increase observed loss but never decreased it;
- barrier magnitude, `A_ref` and interaction `kappa` had much weaker/mixed pooled-loss effects within the matched complete subset.

### Conditions

These matched effects are conditional on candidate completeness and the declared finite factor levels. They do not include how those factors affect source feasibility or completeness and are not universal monotonicity theorems.

### Boundary now being mapped

The rapid-to-persistence frontier in `(kappa_mu,p_star)` space. The coarse grid shows that this frontier shifts toward lower `p_star` as `kappa_mu` increases.

## H-MD-3a — warning-domain evaluability

### Recovered

Under the strict Protocol 002 common candidate family, the eligible set was empty at every coordinate: 15/15 `no_domain_selected`.

This is a negative result for the declared grid/family, not evidence that genetic warning failed and not proof that an evaluable domain cannot exist elsewhere.

### Conditions

`R4` event-regime evaluability requires every one of five seed blocks to show post-baseline functional-loss frequency in `[0.30,0.70]`, without access to genetic-warning fields.

### Boundary now being mapped

Whether R4 is absent structurally or was missed because the original recurrent-transition grid crossed a narrow rapid-to-persistence frontier too coarsely.

The first refinement is prospectively fixed at `kappa_mu=0.05`, `p_star=0.775–0.875`, with two non-transition anchors selected from the warning-blind candidates closest to the strict band.

## H-MD-3b — direction-only effect on warning performance

### Recovered

No matched finite direction-only warning effect has yet been identified, because Protocol 002 supplied no matched R4 domain. Protocol 003 recovered evaluability only by separately recalibrating non-matched eco-genetic domains and therefore provides a portability result rather than a single-factor causal estimate.

### Theoretical constraints already recovered

- direction has no universal signed effect on heterozygosity;
- stronger local high-associated support can coincide with lower heterozygosity;
- fixed-weight among-patch diversity gap contracts with `kappa_mu` independently of `p_star`;
- for a pre-state below a local support threshold, stronger `kappa_mu` lowers the `p_star` required to reach that threshold.

### Condition required before H-MD-3b can be tested

At least one warning-blind, independently confirmed matched R4 interval with non-direction parameters fixed. Warning endpoints remain locked until that prerequisite is met.

## Cross-hypothesis condition hierarchy

The integrated study can therefore be summarized as a sequence of nested conditions:

```text
C0: can a high functional state exist?                 H1 / H-MD-1
        ↓
C1: can fragmentation/deterioration move it?           H3 / H-MD-2
        ↓
C2: is functional loss reproducible but nondegenerate? H-MD-3a / R4
        ↓
C3: does genetic change occur before that loss?        H2 / H-MD-3b
        ↓
C4: is that warning portable across regimes?           Protocol 003 / future tests
```

This nested condition structure is the primary object to recover before final manuscript framing.
