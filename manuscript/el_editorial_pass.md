# Ecology Letters editorial pass

## Editorial decision at this stage

**Promising conceptual manuscript; not yet submission-ready.**

The integrated paper now has a defensible Ecology Letters-level proposition: genetic-warning reliability is generated by the eco-genetic closure linking ecological interaction, demography, inheritance, state turnover, observation, and functional-trait loss. Directional recurrent transition is the perturbation used to expose that dependency, not the sole novelty.

## What now works

1. The paper opens with loss of ecological function before population extinction.
2. The predecessor mechanism and extension evidence form one causal argument without pooling their ledgers.
3. The preregistered no-domain outcome is treated as evidence about event-regime feasibility.
4. The fresh-seed comparison shows a biologically interpretable change in availability, ordering, censoring, and intervention time.
5. Figure 1 and the manuscript title now communicate the same closure-first story.

## Remaining editorial blockers

### 1. Literature support is not yet embedded in the prose

The Introduction and Discussion presently state the right conceptual claims but need selective citations at the exact points where they invoke functional extinction, early warning, genetic monitoring, fragmentation, evolutionary rescue, and mutation or state-transition bias. A long review would weaken the manuscript; the target is a small number of load-bearing references per paragraph.

### 2. Article identity must be chosen before final formatting

The manuscript should be prepared as a concise conceptual/model contribution, not as a protocol paper. Stage labels, workflow history, batch mechanics, and artifact provenance belong in Methods, Supplement, or repository metadata. The selected article type will determine the final word and display-item limits and must be checked against current journal guidance immediately before submission.

### 3. Figures 2–6 still carry an implementation-history inheritance

The figures are scientifically usable, but captions and panel organization should be rewritten around biological questions:

- Where can a high-trait state be reconstructed?
- Which closures generate rapid loss, persistence, or seed heterogeneity?
- When is warning estimable?
- How often does warning lead, tie, lag, or become censored?
- How much intervention time remains?

Protocol identifiers should appear in provenance sentences, not as the reader-facing organizing principle.

### 4. The comparison must not look like a two-point universal test

The symmetric bridge and directional transition are independently calibrated demonstration domains. The manuscript must continue to state that they do not represent the entire 15-coordinate map. The stronger general result comes from combining the full regime map with the two-domain warning comparison.

### 5. Ecological interpretation needs one concrete running example

The paper lists pollination, dispersal, defence, and mutualism. One interaction-dependent trait should be used consistently for intuition without implying empirical parameterization. A plant–pollinator or host–symbiont example would make the distinction among population persistence, allele persistence, realised trait expression, and ecological function immediately legible.

## Recommended next production sequence

1. Insert and verify the minimal citation set using the existing citation map.
2. Rewrite all six figure captions and decide which displays remain in the main paper.
3. Synchronize main-text figure calls, Supplement numbering, and machine-readable table captions.
4. Add author metadata, contributions, funding, conflicts, acknowledgements, and data/code statements.
5. Rebuild the submission bundle from the merged head and archive a frozen release.
6. Draft a cover letter centred on the conceptual advance and why it matters for function-first conservation monitoring.

## Claims that must remain locked

- Genetic diversity erosion **can** precede functional-trait loss; it is not claimed to do so universally.
- `p_mu*` is an effective transition equilibrium, not an empirical biological mutation-rate estimate.
- Endpoint summaries share trajectories and are not independent replicates.
- Protocol 003 is separately declared and is not a retrospective repair of Protocol 002.
- Parent and extension trajectories are not pooled.
- `no_domain_selected` is an event-regime and design-feasibility result, not evidence that warning failed.
