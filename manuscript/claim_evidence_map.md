# Claim–evidence map

This file is the manuscript claim gate. Detailed numerical provenance lives in `artifact_index.md`; the condition hierarchy lives in `docs/HYPOTHESIS_PROGRAM.md`.

Parent and extension repositories are separate computational provenance units and their trajectories are never pooled. The pinned parent scientific commit is `dd8ee379d0d3518194c767d16402042525bc00dc`.

**Phase-J interpretation rule:** the historical R4 label is a finite-panel all-block calibration certificate under a specified sampling design. It is not by itself a sample-size-invariant biological regime. Biological claims should target the distribution of functional-loss events—central incidence plus among-block heterogeneity/tails—before referring to any finite-panel certificate.

## Prohibited claims

Do not claim that:

- the historical 15/15 `no_domain_selected` result proves a finite R4 certificate cannot exist;
- R4 itself means genetic warning succeeds;
- R4 is a sample-size-invariant biological regime;
- the exact `m=.10` Phase-E R3 label is a portable connectivity boundary;
- adding more seed blocks should leave an all-pass R4 certificate unchanged;
- finer `p_star`, connectivity, kappa, partner weights, rewiring strength, pollen fraction, seed selection or R4-threshold tuning is justified merely to obtain a desired categorical result;
- legacy `migration_rate` is demographic, pollinator, pollen, seed or recolonisation movement in general;
- the narrow Phase-I `g↔m/2` identity holds for arbitrary pollen kernels or other movement processes;
- connectivity universally rescues or harms function;
- Phase F proves interaction support is irrelevant;
- interaction `kappa` is partner richness, connectance, pollinator diversity or network dimensionality;
- Phase G is a full network experiment or shows partner loss universally raises mean risk;
- Phase H shows rewiring is universally ineffective;
- recovery of connectance or aggregate interaction support proves recovery of the functional-loss distribution;
- Phase I proves pollen spatial kernel is universally irrelevant;
- recurrent-transition direction alone caused the Stage III warning difference;
- `p_star` is an empirical mutation rate;
- genetic diversity is a monotone proxy for local functional support;
- endpoint rows sharing trajectories are independent biological replicates;
- finite Type S results are universal ecological thresholds or theorems.
