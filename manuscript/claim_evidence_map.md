## Interpretation rule for historical R1–R4 labels

R1–R4 remain immutable labels from the preregistered warning-blind screen. They are **not** latent biological classes. Historical R3 is described as a mixed-block screen failure unless independent high-precision evidence supports excess between-block heterogeneity. Historical R4 means every observed block lay within the fixed intermediate-incidence band; it does not itself prove warning success or biological exchangeability.

## Prohibited claims

Do not claim that:

- the historical 15/15 `no_domain_selected` result proves an intermediate-incidence domain cannot exist;
- R4 itself means genetic warning succeeds, or R3 itself demonstrates biological seed heterogeneity;
- `m=.10` is a reproducible connectivity threshold or that connectivity universally rescues/harms function;
- `migration_rate` is demographic, pollinator, pollen, seed or recolonisation movement;
- Phase U proves no future seed ensemble could show `m=.10` heterogeneity;
- interaction `kappa` is partner richness, connectance, pollinator diversity or network simplification;
- reduced-form partner layers are a full dynamic network, or that Phase T proves partner dynamics are universally irrelevant;
- cross-layer alignment has a universal signed effect on long-term functional loss; the declared finite incidence contrast was not significant;
- matching layer-wise means/marginals, occupancy or standard neutral-diversity summaries establishes the same functional-fragmentation regime;
- the fresh strict warning replication establishes a universal 5%, 10% or 20% threshold outside the frozen symmetric domain;
- relative warning success makes the failed absolute thresholds robust;
- recurrent-transition direction alone caused historical Protocol-003 differences;
- a direction-only warning effect has been tested under a matched loss process; the required same-strength comparator was absent;
- finer `p_star` or retuned ecology should be searched post hoc to manufacture a direction comparator;
- `p_star` is an empirically estimated mutation rate;
- genetic diversity is a monotone proxy for local functional support;
- urban and island systems are ecological equivalents or already shown to share a regime;
- endpoint rows sharing trajectories are independent biological replicates;
- finite Type S results are universal ecological thresholds or theorems.
