# Nature Ecology & Evolution flagship target audit — 2026-09-05

## Decision

Develop the EG grand synthesis as a **Nature Ecology & Evolution Article first shot** before releasing the four overlapping standalone manuscripts to separate journals.

This is a high-risk/high-upside route. The four submission-ready component packages remain frozen fallback assets; they are not to be simultaneously submitted while a substantially overlapping flagship is under editorial or peer review.

## Current NEE format and editorial bar

The current journal scope is ecology and evolution broadly, with professional editors selecting a small fraction of submissions representing accomplished and significant advances. Primary research is the journal's main focus. The Article format is designed for a substantial novel study with a complex story involving several techniques or approaches, with up to 3,500 words of main text, a 200-word abstract, up to six main display items, and up to ten Extended Data items.

This format fits the proposed synthesis better than four concatenated short papers because the flagship combines:

1. exact analytical results;
2. prospectively locked finite-model experiments;
3. a full-denominator predictive-warning audit;
4. seven locked natural-data analyses with distinct measurement/representation outcomes.

## Editorial question

The paper must answer one question that is broader than eco-genetic fragmentation:

> **What must be valid before ecological deterioration can be forecast?**

The answer is a predictive-validity hierarchy:

`biological state -> analytical representation -> predictive signal -> empirical measurement -> future target`

Each mapping can fail independently; success at one layer does not license the next.

## Load-bearing results

### 1. Biological state is not a disturbance label

The EGC fragmentation gradient establishes that a common structural change need not move potential viability, realised occupancy, interaction support, effective size and realised trait mass along one common deterioration axis. Potential high-trait viability changed from 1,037/1,037 at one patch to 0/1,037 at every tested subdivision while realised occupancy persisted at approximately 99.6–100% at generation 30.

### 2. Matching summaries can hide different futures

The aligned/anti-aligned constructive pair holds census, interaction, allele-frequency and trait marginals, H_alpha, H_gamma and F_ST fixed while reversing cross-layer alignment. The exact next interaction transition differs by as much as 0.2543. In a separately prospectively locked 1,500-pair propagation experiment, anti-aligned minus aligned functional-loss risk was 0.0 percentage points at generation 5, +0.33 at 10, +5.33 at 20 and +5.20 at 40.

### 3. Perfect temporal precedence can have zero discrimination

Six frozen H_alpha/H_gamma thresholds preceded all observed losses in two independently seeded ensembles (35/35 and 33/33), yet the same thresholds fired in every horizon non-event (48/48 and 49/49), yielding sensitivity 1, specificity 0 and binary-marker AUC 0.5. An exact denominator result shows why event-conditioned precedence cannot identify specificity.

### 4. Natural proxies fail at different upstream gates

Seven natural-data analyses distinguish measurement adequacy, representation preservation, residual-context testing and cross-study identifiability. The natural evidence is not used as validation of the finite simulator and is not pooled into one ecological effect.

## Why this is one Article rather than four chapters

The synthesis is organized around four constructive non-implications:

1. common disturbance != one biological state;
2. matching marginals != matching futures;
3. perfect precedence != predictive discrimination;
4. plausible proxy != validated empirical state.

These jointly support a positive general claim:

> **Ecological predictability belongs to a validated chain of state, representation, prediction target and measurement, not to any familiar summary or early signal in isolation.**

## Main display ceiling

Use five main figures and no main table unless needed:

1. predictive-validity hierarchy;
2. EGC biological-state separation;
3. EGWE constructive representation certificate + propagation;
4. warning precedence-versus-discrimination;
5. EGWEE seven-system gate map.

Move detailed theorem proofs, operator-portability negatives, six-system inference-boundary table and full per-system evidence into Methods / Extended Data / Supplementary Information.

## Main editorial risks

### Risk A — reads as four unrelated studies

Mitigation: every Results section must answer one layer of the same forecast-validity hierarchy, and Figure 1 must define the hierarchy before individual evidence is shown.

### Risk B — model-to-nature bridge is indirect

Mitigation: state explicitly that the natural analyses test measurement obligations predicted by the hierarchy, not external validity of the simulator. Do not claim end-to-end natural forecast sufficiency.

### Risk C — too specialist / eco-genetic

Mitigation: title, abstract and Introduction must begin with ecological prediction, not genetics, fragmentation or the repository programme. Genetic diversity and fragmentation become worked examples of a general inference problem.

### Risk D — overlap with fallback papers

Mitigation: do not simultaneously submit substantially overlapping component manuscripts. The standalone packages remain internal fallbacks until the flagship receives an editorial decision or is withdrawn.

## Dream ceiling

Nature Ecology & Evolution is a defensible high-risk first shot now because the Article format explicitly accommodates a complex multi-approach story and the journal currently publishes major ecological forecasting work. A direct prospective natural state-to-future bridge would raise the probability of success but is not made a prerequisite for assembling and testing the present Article.

If NEE declines at editorial assessment, next integrated routes are Nature Communications / Science Advances depending on the reason for rejection; if the criticism is that the evidence modes are too heterogeneous rather than insufficiently broad, revert promptly to the four claim-specific submission packages rather than endlessly expanding the flagship.
