# EG grand synthesis dependency audit — 2026-09-05

## Decision

The four current EG publication lanes should remain submission-ready as independent manuscripts, but they can also be treated as four empirical/theoretical tests of one larger question:

> **What must be preserved, observed, and validated before ecological deterioration is forecastable?**

The candidate flagship is not a causal chain `fragmentation -> missing alignment -> warning failure -> natural residual`. That chain is not supported. The defensible integration is a **predictive-validity stack** in which four logically distinct mappings can fail.

## Predictive-validity stack

Let `X_t` be the complete biological state under a declared dynamic closure, `Z_t = phi(X_t)` an analytical state representation, `W_t = g(Z_0:t)` a candidate warning statistic, and `Y_(t+h)` a declared future endpoint. In natural data, let `M_t` denote the actual measurement process used to estimate a candidate state coordinate.

A forecast claim requires at least four distinct obligations:

1. **Biological-state distinction / ontology** — the biological objects relevant to the target must not be collapsed merely because they respond to the same upstream disturbance.
2. **Representation adequacy** — the supplied representation `phi(X_t)` must preserve distinctions that matter for the declared target and horizon.
3. **Predictive validation** — an early marker `W_t` must discriminate future events from comparable non-events; event-conditioned temporal precedence alone is insufficient.
4. **Measurement and identifiability** — in natural data, the measured candidate state must earn endpoint relevance, preserve its intended information through preprocessing, and support the requested residual or cross-study inference.

No success at an earlier layer licenses a later layer automatically.

## Four non-implications that can become one paper

The strongest unified structure is a sequence of constructive non-implications:

1. **Common disturbance does not imply one biological state.** Fragmentation can separate potential viability, realised occupancy, interaction support, effective size and trait mass.
2. **Matching common state summaries does not imply matching futures.** Cross-layer alignment can change the exact transition and horizon-dependent functional-loss risk while the usual marginals remain identical.
3. **Perfect temporal precedence does not imply prediction.** A marker can precede every event while firing in every non-event and therefore have chance discrimination.
4. **A plausible natural proxy does not automatically earn state status.** Measurement inadequacy, representation collapse, missing coordinates and cross-study confounding can block downstream interpretation.

These are not four slices of one statistical result. They are four failures of implication in the same forecasting pipeline.

## Candidate one-paper thesis

> **Ecological predictability is a property of a validated chain from biological state, through representation and measurement, to a declared future target—not of a disturbance label, summary statistic, or temporally early marker in isolation.**

Possible reader-facing titles:

- **What makes ecological deterioration predictable? A hierarchy from biological state to warning validity**
- **Prediction requires the right state: from fragmentation to representation, warning and measurement**
- **From state to warning: four failure points in ecological prediction**
- **Ecological forecasts fail when state, representation, signal and measurement are conflated**

## Why the current evidence is not yet a complete end-to-end flagship

The model papers form a coherent internal evidence chain, but the natural-data programme is deliberately independent. Current natural systems illustrate measurement, missing-coordinate, representation and identifiability boundaries; they do **not** prospectively validate the synthetic model's complete state or show that conditioning on an empirically measured joint state removes origin/history effects over repeated future windows.

Therefore a single flagship written now must be framed as a general predictive-validity hierarchy supported by multiple constructive counterexamples and natural-data diagnostics. It must not claim an end-to-end empirical validation from fragmentation to warning.

## Decisive bridge that would raise the ceiling

A future flagship becomes substantially stronger if one prospective natural system supplies a synchronized repeated state-to-future design with:

1. upstream structural/environmental context;
2. local resource/demographic support;
3. realised interaction state;
4. partner or mating/pollen connectivity;
5. relevant trait/genetic coordinates and cross-layer alignment;
6. a declared future functional/reproductive endpoint;
7. repeated future windows or an independent validation cohort;
8. a candidate early marker evaluated on both event and non-event units.

The decisive comparison would be nested held-out prediction:

`context-only -> coarse marginals -> joint/process state -> warning statistic -> joint state + warning -> + origin/history`.

The flagship claim would be strengthened if (a) joint/process state improves future prediction beyond coarse summaries, (b) warning performance is evaluated on the full denominator, and (c) residual origin/history is tested only after the measured state has earned endpoint relevance. A failure at any stage remains informative under the same hierarchy.

## Publication strategy

### Track A — preserve the four ready papers

Keep the existing submission packages intact. They are claim-specific, reproducible, and do not borrow unsupported causal links from one another.

### Track B — develop the optional flagship independently

Do not merge or delete the four source manuscripts. Build the flagship as a new manuscript that cites/derives from the same locked evidence but owns only the higher-order hierarchy. This prevents a high-risk flagship attempt from delaying or destroying the independent papers.

## Venue ceiling

### Current evidence, no new natural bridge

- **Ecology Letters:** realistic high-end target if the manuscript is concise and centered on the four non-implications / predictive-validity hierarchy rather than repository history.
- **Nature Communications:** plausible stretch if the integrated framework is made broadly accessible, the multiple evidence modes are tightly unified, and the natural analyses are presented as independent tests of inferential layers rather than miscellaneous case studies.
- **Science Advances:** plausible but high-risk stretch if the paper is made genuinely interdisciplinary around prediction/validation rather than a specialist eco-genetic model story.
- **Nature Ecology & Evolution:** dream-level, but current evidence is likely short of a direct end-to-end natural validation. The journal explicitly seeks the most accomplished and significant advances across ecology/evolution, so a hierarchy supported mainly by synthetic model counterexamples plus heterogeneous secondary-data diagnostics would face a high editorial bar.

### With one decisive prospective natural bridge

A clean end-to-end natural test that measures joint state, full-denominator warning performance and held-out future function would make **Nature Ecology & Evolution** a credible first-shot dream target. **Science Advances** and **Nature Communications** would also become strong high-end alternatives.

### Nature / Science ceiling

The current programme should not be planned around Nature or Science. A credible attempt would require a much broader empirical demonstration that the predictive-validity hierarchy changes understanding or practice across multiple natural systems, with an unusually simple and surprising central result. This is beyond the present evidence and should not drive current analysis choices.

## Submission-order implication

Until an end-to-end natural bridge exists, the four independent manuscripts remain the lower-risk publication route. The optional flagship should be developed in parallel as a synthesis of **non-implications and validation layers**, not by concatenating the four current manuscripts.

## No-go rules for the flagship

- Do not claim fragmentation caused the aligned/anti-aligned contrast unless tested.
- Do not claim omitted cross-layer alignment caused the warning thresholds to fail.
- Do not claim the natural datasets validate the synthetic closure.
- Do not call negative residual context evidence of complete state measurement.
- Do not pool the seven natural systems onto one ecological effect-size axis.
- Do not treat generation 20 as a universal timescale.
- Do not reopen frozen warning thresholds or natural endpoints to make the hierarchy look cleaner.
