# NEE flagship evidence-role map — 2026-09-06

## Decision

The flagship must not present Miyake-jima *Camellia–Zosterops*, *Crepis sancta*, *Conospermum undulatum*, *Spondias purpurea*, Honshu–Izu, Zurich, Toronto, Oenothera, Eschscholzia, Mallorca carob or *Campanula americana* as independent external validation of the finite eco-genetic mechanism.

The evidence hierarchy is:

### A. Load-bearing tests of the flagship claim

1. **EGC / eco-genetic-criticality** — theorem-guided state separation under fragmentation.
   - interaction state, potential viability, realised occupancy, effective size and genetic summaries are distinct biological objects;
   - the locked fragmentation gradient shows that they do not share one response trajectory.

2. **EGWE state-validity** — constructive joint-state test.
   - declared layer-wise marginals are held fixed;
   - only cross-layer spatial alignment changes;
   - exact next transition differs, and the separately locked propagation experiment shows later functional-risk divergence.

3. **EGWE warning-validity** — full-denominator warning test.
   - six frozen diversity thresholds retain perfect temporal precedence among losses;
   - the same rules fire in every non-event;
   - therefore temporal erosion of a component can be biologically real without identifying functional fate.

These three evidence blocks carry the mechanistic and inferential conclusions of the flagship.

### B. Empirical projection / measurement stress tests

The EGWEE natural-data analyses are not external validation of the synthetic closure. Their role in the flagship is narrower:

- show what kinds of empirical coordinates would have to be measured if the joint-state interpretation is taken seriously;
- expose missing process coordinates, measurement inadequacy and representation collapse in real archives;
- demonstrate that coarse geography/context can sometimes add little after a more proximal functional state, without proving state completeness;
- define prospective measurement obligations for a future end-to-end natural test.

If included in the flagship, these analyses belong in a compact Discussion / Extended Data section labelled **empirical projection** or **measurement consequences**, not as a fourth coequal mechanistic Result.

### C. Literature examples for ecological interpretation

The following systems are literature anchors only. They illustrate biological routes that the theory makes salient; they are not validation datasets for the theory.

#### Montpellier *Crepis sancta* — uncompensated local functional loss

Published work shows low flowering density associated with reduced pollinator activity and reproductive function, with no strong autonomous-selfing rescue despite nonzero wider pollen/seed movement.

Use in Discussion:

> local support loss can propagate to interaction and function when compensatory routes are insufficient.

Do not write that *Crepis* verifies the simulator or the aligned/anti-aligned mechanism.

#### Miyake-jima *Camellia japonica–Zosterops japonicus* — movement-mediated compensation

Published work after volcanic disturbance shows reduced local floral support but broader bird movement, increased pollen immigration/mixing and maintained or enhanced pollination components.

Use in Discussion:

> local resource loss need not imply functional collapse when partner movement reorganizes the interaction process in a compensating direction.

This is a real-world counterpoint to monotone fragmentation narratives, not a validation of any model migration parameter.

#### Perth *Conospermum undulatum* — temporal/cohort mismatch

Published work shows contemporary pollen-flow and reproductive deterioration while adult neutral genetic structure retains historical connectivity.

Use in Discussion:

> components of the eco-genetic state can be temporally misaligned; standing adult genetics may describe ecological memory rather than the current loss-generating process.

Do not claim that the warning audit predicts this exact natural lag.

#### *Spondias purpurea* — coordinated interaction/connectivity/function deterioration

Published work jointly reports reduced visitation, contracted pollen flow, reduced sire diversity, lower reproductive function and stronger genetic deterioration in younger cohorts than adults under fragmentation.

Use in Discussion:

> a natural system can show multiple process layers moving in the same deterioration direction, making cohort-specific state measurement especially important.

Do not present it as an external replication of the finite closure.

### D. Urban–island comparison — conceptual projection, not a pooled test

The urban–island programme asks:

> Do different fragmentation mechanisms generate comparable future-relevant coordination states, despite different upstream causes?

The current archives do not identify a direct shared urban–island law because state semantics, responses, taxa, protocols and study identity are confounded.

Honshu–Izu and Zurich provide bounded within-system observations that coarse context variables can become predictively redundant after a more proximal process state is supplied. They do **not** establish

`P(F | S, island) = P(F | S, urban)`.

Their value for the flagship is to motivate the ecological projection:

- `urban`, `island`, `forest fragment` and `volcanic disturbance` are upstream histories;
- the scientific object of interest is the process state they produce;
- future comparative work should match interaction, movement/mating, function, cohort and spatial-alignment measurements across origins.

## Positive ecological interpretation

The flagship should not lead with a list of invalidities. The positive ecological statement is:

> **Functional vulnerability is relational rather than purely compositional: ecological function depends on whether interaction support and trait/genetic support remain coordinated in the places and times where function is produced.**

The current model directly supports only the declared cross-layer spatial-alignment version of this statement. Broader movement, mating, rewiring and cohort interpretations are ecological projections motivated by the literature examples above.

## Discussion structure

1. **Hidden functional vulnerability** — function-supporting conditions can diverge from persistence and common summaries.
2. **Joint spatial coordination as a state variable** — matched marginals can still yield different futures.
3. **Why marginal warning variables can be insufficient** — perfect erosion timing need not identify functional fate.
4. **Projection to nature** — literature examples show collapse, compensation and temporal lag as biologically plausible routes.
5. **Urban and island systems as contrasting upstream routes** — not as ecological equivalents and not as a currently identified shared law.
6. **Prospective empirical test** — measure the same process coordinates and future function in replicated systems under matched protocols.

## Claim firewall

Never write:
- `natural examples validate the model`;
- `Miyake proves movement rescues the modelled state`;
- `Crepis proves cross-layer misalignment`;
- `Conospermum validates the warning result`;
- `Spondias replicates the simulator`;
- `urban and island systems are the same regime`.

Allowed:
- `the model makes these published natural patterns newly interpretable as distinct routes through a broader coordination problem`;
- `the natural literature motivates which processes should be measured in an end-to-end test`;
- `the examples demonstrate ecological plausibility of collapse, compensation and lag without constituting external validation of the synthetic closure`.
