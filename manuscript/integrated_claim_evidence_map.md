## Required wording

Use:

- “can precede,” not “always precedes”;
- “mutation or effective state-transition direction,” when discussing biological interpretation;
- “warning availability and reliability,” not only “warning performance”;
- “functional-trait loss,” where the distinction from population extinction matters;
- “finite Type S evidence for the declared closure,” in figure captions and limitations.

Avoid:

- describing `p_mu*` as an empirical mutation rate;
- treating the six endpoint counts as independent samples;
- combining parent and extension trajectories into a pooled statistical test;
- presenting Protocol 002 no-domain as a failed simulation;
- presenting Protocol 003 as a retrospective modification of the parent protocol;
- claiming that connectivity, directional mutation, or genetic diversity is universally beneficial or harmful.

## Novelty test

The integrated paper is novel only if the main narrative preserves all three links:

1. ecological fragmentation and interaction state create the possibility of genetic warning;
2. mutation direction controls the event regimes in which warning can be evaluated;
3. independently calibrated directional transition changes valid-pair availability, ordering, and lead time.

Removing link 1 reduces the work to a mutation sensitivity study. Removing links 2–3 reduces it to the predecessor's conditional warning result. The integrated novelty is the causal connection across all three.
