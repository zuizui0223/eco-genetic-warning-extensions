# NEE editor / reviewer stress test — 2026-09-05

## Decision standard

The flagship is worth a Nature Ecology & Evolution first shot only if it reads as **one general advance about ecological predictability**, not as four internally coherent papers compressed into one long manuscript.

The decisive test is therefore not whether every component result is strong. It is whether removing any one of the four validation layers weakens one common conclusion:

> **Ecological prediction requires a validated chain from biological state, through target-relevant representation and full-denominator signal validation, to empirically adequate measurement.**

The current draft passes that conceptual test, but five predictable editorial/reviewer objections remain.

---

## Objection 1 — “This is four studies joined by a conceptual diagram.”

### Why an editor could say this

The evidence modes are heterogeneous:

- EGC theorem + fragmentation simulation;
- EGWE matched-state representation + propagation;
- warning denominator theorem/audit;
- seven independent natural-data analyses.

A chronological or repository-based presentation would look like a research programme summary rather than one Article.

### Current defence

The Article is not organized by repository, chronology or hypothesis number. It is organized by four constructive non-implications in one forecast-validity stack:

1. common disturbance != one biological state;
2. matching marginals != matching futures;
3. perfect precedence != predictive discrimination;
4. plausible proxy != validated empirical state.

Figure 1 defines this hierarchy before any component result. Figures 2–5 each answer exactly one obligation in that hierarchy.

### Remaining requirement

Do not add historical campaign detail to the main text merely because it is scientifically interesting. If a paragraph does not strengthen one of the four non-implications or the positive hierarchy, move it to Methods / Extended Data / source paper.

**Current risk:** medium, manageable.

---

## Objection 2 — “The integration is philosophical rather than experimentally connected.”

### Why an editor could say this

The paper does not demonstrate the causal chain:

`fragmentation -> cross-layer anti-alignment -> warning failure -> natural residual context`.

Nor should it. Those links were not tested.

### Current defence

The paper explicitly rejects that causal reading. The integration is one of **inferential prerequisites**. Each result establishes that one commonly assumed implication fails, so the next predictive claim requires an additional validation step.

This is analogous to a measurement/identification hierarchy: the layers are logically ordered without claiming that the observed failure at layer 1 mechanically causes the observed failure at layer 2.

### Remaining requirement

Keep the phrase **“validation obligations, not a causal mediation chain”** visible in Figure 1, Discussion and cover letter. Do not use transition phrases such as “therefore alignment explains warning failure.”

**Current risk:** medium.

---

## Objection 3 — “The strongest results are synthetic; the natural data do not validate the theory.”

### Why an editor could say this

The 0.2543 transition certificate and 1,500-pair propagation result come from a finite declared closure. The seven natural systems are heterogeneous secondary/public datasets and do not measure the synthetic state end to end.

### Current defence

The Article does **not** claim external validation of the simulator. The natural-data section tests a different prediction obligation: whether candidate measurements and representations earn the state role required for downstream inference.

The fact that natural systems stop at different gates is itself the empirical result. The natural evidence therefore broadens the hierarchy without pretending to reproduce the synthetic mechanism.

### Ceiling-raising experiment

The prospective Cirsium bridge is the single clearest future addition. In one natural system it would compare:

`context -> coarse marginals -> joint/process state -> warning -> joint state + warning -> + origin/history`

against a held-out future reproductive endpoint and full event/non-event denominator.

A successful bridge would materially strengthen NEE prospects. A prospectively failed bridge would still provide a valid gate result.

### Submission decision

Do not delay the current first-shot Article automatically for this bridge. Assemble the current manuscript and judge its editorial coherence first. Integrate the bridge only if field timing allows it without retrospective redesign.

**Current risk:** high — this is the principal scientific ceiling on the present flagship.

---

## Objection 4 — “This is mainly a collection of negative results.”

### Why an editor could say this

Several branches are framed as failures:

- one state label fails;
- coarse summaries fail;
- warning thresholds fail;
- proxies or representations fail.

### Current defence

There are two clear positive results:

1. a constructive state distinction changes the exact next transition by 0.2543 and propagates to an approximately five-percentage-point loss-risk difference at generations 20 and 40 under the declared path;
2. the four non-implications jointly define a positive predictive-validity hierarchy with explicit testable obligations.

The warning result is also sharper than a generic null: perfect temporal precedence reaches the exact worst-discrimination endpoint permitted by sensitivity one. Oenothera positively identifies a missing contemporary coordinate.

### Remaining requirement

Abstract, title, Figure 1 and first Discussion paragraph must lead with the **positive hierarchy**, not with “four ways prediction fails.” Use failure results as proofs that the hierarchy is necessary.

**Current risk:** low-to-medium after reframing.

---

## Objection 5 — “Why Nature Ecology & Evolution rather than a specialist forecasting or methods journal?”

### Why an editor could say this

No new forecasting algorithm is introduced. The system is eco-genetic and pollination-heavy. The hierarchy could be mistaken for methodological hygiene rather than a biological advance.

### Current defence

The biological result is that predictive equivalence cannot be inferred from familiar ecological/genetic summaries or a structural disturbance label. The state relevant to prediction is target-, horizon- and operator-dependent, and this is demonstrated constructively rather than asserted as best practice.

The manuscript connects ecology/evolutionary state, fragmentation, multilayer organization, genetic diversity, ecological function, warning signals and empirical pollination/mating systems. It therefore addresses how ecological and evolutionary information becomes predictive, not merely how to score a model.

### Remaining requirement

Keep genetics as one evidence layer, not the paper's identity. The title should remain generic. Do not lead the Introduction with fragmentation, H_alpha/H_gamma or simulator architecture.

**Current risk:** medium.

# Reviewer-level technical attacks

## A. “Generation 20 was selected because it became significant.”

**Answer:** false. Horizons 5, 10, 20 and 40 and the 1,500-pair primary cohort were prospectively locked. The paper reports the entire curve and explicitly forbids treating generation 20 as a universal cutoff.

## B. “The 1,500-pair result contradicts the original generation-60 p=.143 result.”

**Answer:** no. The original 500-pair generation-60 contrast was +4.4 percentage points with a wide paired interval. The later experiment is a separately locked precision/horizon study; nested 500/1000/1500 prefixes show interval contraction around similar generation-20/40 effect magnitudes rather than qualitative reversal.

## C. “Specificity zero is trivial because thresholds were too weak.”

**Answer:** the manuscript does not claim the six thresholds exhaust warning design. The general result is the denominator theorem: perfect event-conditioned precedence cannot identify specificity. The frozen ensembles instantiate the sharp AUC=0.5 endpoint without post-result threshold search.

## D. “A negative residual-context result proves nothing.”

**Answer:** agreed and explicitly stated. It is conditional evidence under a supplied partial state, not proof of completeness. That distinction is one of the empirical gate results.

## E. “Campanula just shows that z-scoring removes scale.”

**Answer:** the statistical identity itself is simple. Its role is empirical representation audit: a biologically motivated efficiency weighting was present in raw predictors but the prospectively declared feature-wise standardization made the richer and simpler representations machine-identical. The claim is not that standardization is generally bad.

## F. “Seven natural systems are cherry-picked.”

**Answer:** the flagship inherits the locked gate programme, including STOP and failed-access/identifiability branches. The systems are not selected to estimate one effect and are not pooled. Their role is to instantiate distinct outcomes of the same prospective interpretation sequence. The source manifest and gate registry must remain available to reviewers.

## Go / no-go rule

**GO for a Nature Ecology & Evolution first shot** if final Figure 1–5 visual QA makes the paper read as one hierarchy and no new unsupported causal language enters the manuscript.

Do **not** respond to a desk rejection by adding arbitrary analyses. Route according to the rejection reason:

- `insufficient general significance / needs direct natural validation` -> consider the prospective Cirsium bridge or Nature Communications;
- `too heterogeneous / four stories` -> split immediately back to the frozen standalone manuscripts;
- `scope but scientifically strong` -> Nature Communications / Science Advances depending on editorial feedback;
- `specific technical criticism` -> address only if it concerns a load-bearing claim.
