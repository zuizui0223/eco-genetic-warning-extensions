# Cover letter draft — Ecology Letters

Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, **“Genetic warning emerges from eco-genetic closure in fragmented systems,”** as a Letter in *Ecology Letters*.

Conservation monitoring increasingly seeks signals that precede the loss of ecological function rather than population extinction alone. Genetic diversity is often treated as a portable warning statistic, but the ecological and genetic processes that generate a diversity trajectory also determine whether functional-loss events occur, whether warning thresholds are observable, and how much intervention time remains. We formalize this dependency as **eco-genetic closure**.

We combine a theorem-guided finite eco-genetic model of interaction feedback and fragmentation with an independently declared directional recurrent-transition extension. A symmetric benchmark produced diversity erosion before all observed functional-trait-loss events. Across a broader transition map, however, warning-blind calibration separated systems into rapid-loss, persistence, and seed-heterogeneous regimes and found no common eligible validation domain under the preregistered family. Two independently calibrated demonstration domains then showed that changing transition closure altered warning availability, censoring, ordering, and lead time. Thus, warning reliability was not an intrinsic property of the diversity statistic.

We believe the study fits *Ecology Letters* because its primary contribution is ecological and conceptual. It identifies event-regime feasibility and biological calibration as components of early-warning theory, distinguishes functional-trait loss from numerical extinction, and explains why warning thresholds should not be transferred among systems without evidence that their eco-genetic closures are comparable. Mutation or effective state-transition direction is used as a controlled perturbation of closure, not presented as the sole novelty or as an empirical mutation-rate estimate.

The manuscript is concise, hypothesis-driven, and prepared as a Letter. Its main display allocation contains six figures and no main-text tables or text boxes. Parent and extension simulations remain separate evidence ledgers; endpoint summaries sharing trajectories are explicitly treated as correlated repeated summaries rather than independent replicates. All numerical results, protocol boundaries, censoring rules, and immutable artifact records are available in the accompanying repositories and submission bundle.
