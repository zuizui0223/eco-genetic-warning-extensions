# Cover letter draft — Ecology Letters

Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, **“Joint state representation and empirical measurement gates define eco-genetic predictability,”** as a Letter in *Ecology Letters*.

Conservation monitoring increasingly seeks signals that precede realised functional-trait loss rather than numerical population disappearance alone. Yet a genetic statistic can only serve as an early warning when the underlying eco-genetic state generates an independently identifiable functional-loss process. We treat this as a question of **eco-genetic closure**: which future-relevant ecological and genetic state coordinates must be fixed before warning validation is well posed?

We combine a theorem-guided finite multipatch model with prospectively declared robustness, replication and natural-data tests. Fragmentation reduced interaction support, local effective size and realised high-trait mass before demographic disappearance. Warning-blind calibration recovered loss-generating states while showing that a historical allele-mixing heterogeneity signal did not freshly replicate and was not portable across more explicit movement closures. Under the declared Markov model, the complete present state is future-sufficient, whereas coarse ecological and genetic summaries can hide dynamically important cross-layer spatial alignment.

A full-denominator audit supplies an important negative boundary. All six `H_alpha/H_gamma` thresholds led the 35/35 inherited and 33/33 fresh observed losses, but the same thresholds fired in all 48/48 and 49/49 non-event trajectories. Full-horizon specificity was zero and binary-marker AUC was 0.5. We therefore retain temporal ordering but withdraw predictive early-warning validity rather than selecting a new threshold after outcomes were known: reliability is **not an intrinsic property of the diversity statistic**.

The natural-data extension tests the empirical counterpart of this argument. Across independent systems, upstream geographic or urban context could become predictively redundant after an informative proximal state was supplied, remain necessary when a relevant mating/connectivity coordinate was omitted, or fail to qualify as state information because the measured proxy lacked endpoint relevance. A further *Campanula* analysis showed a distinct failure mode: mechanistic efficiency information was present in the measurements but erased by feature-wise standardization. These results motivate an ordered empirical gate — **measurement adequacy -> representation/information preservation -> residual origin/history test** — before different fragmentation histories are treated as dynamically equivalent.

We believe the study fits *Ecology Letters* because its primary contribution is **ecological and conceptual rather than a model sensitivity analysis**. It shows constructively why future-relevant state can require cross-layer joint representation, and turns that result into an empirical measurement/representation gate. Warning, negative and non-identifiable results are retained as explicit boundaries rather than tuned toward a preferred outcome.

The manuscript is prepared as a Letter with six main figures and no main-text tables or text boxes.
