# Manuscript workspace

**Working title:** *Genetic warning emerges from eco-genetic closure in fragmented systems*

## Paper identity

This manuscript integrates the conceptual and numerical evidence from `zuizui0223/eco-genetic-criticality` and `zuizui0223/eco-genetic-warning-extensions` while preserving the two repositories and their evidence ledgers as separate provenance units. The predecessor supplies the theorem-guided ecological mechanism and the conditional symmetric-closure benchmark. The extension supplies independently declared tests of how recurrent mutation or effective state-transition direction reorganizes source feasibility, trait-loss regimes, warning availability, ordering, and lead time.

The repositories are not being silently merged. Parent trajectories remain parent evidence; extension trajectories remain extension evidence. Their integration occurs at the level of the ecological argument, claim–evidence map, figures, and manuscript.

## Central conceptual claim

Genetic-warning reliability is not a portable property of a diversity statistic. It emerges from the full eco-genetic closure linking fragmentation, interaction state, realised functional-trait occupancy, local effective size, recurrent state turnover, deterioration, calibration, and observation.

Mutation or effective state-transition direction is one mechanism that reshapes this closure. At fixed mutation-map relaxation strength, changing the transition equilibrium partitioned the declared finite system into rapid-loss, persistent, and seed-heterogeneous regimes. Under Protocol 002, no common warning-validation domain satisfied the preregistered rule. A separately declared Protocol 003 recovered two independently calibrated domains and showed different warning availability and ordering: the symmetric bridge produced 323 leads, one tie, and zero lags, whereas the directional transition produced 184 leads, five ties, and 12 lags with stronger censoring and shorter median positive lead time.

## Claims not made

- This paper does not establish a universal genetic-warning theorem.
- It does not estimate biological mutation rates.
- `p_mu*` is an effective recurrent-transition equilibrium, not a species-specific mutation estimate.
- `no_domain_selected` is not interpreted as absence of ecological change or as warning failure.
- Protocol 003 results are not retroactively attributed to Protocol 002 or to the predecessor ledger.
- Endpoint-level counts are correlated within trajectories and are not treated as independent replicates.
- Finite-model results remain Type S evidence for the declared closures.

## Main-text structure

1. **Introduction** — ecological function may disappear before population extinction; warning must therefore be understood through the system that generates both the signal and the endpoint.
2. **Eco-genetic closure** — fragmentation, interaction feedback, trait-state stability, local effective size, diversity, and recurrent state turnover.
3. **Independent evidence design** — source reconstruction, warning-blind calibration, censoring-aware validation, and explicit protocol boundaries.
4. **Fragmentation establishes an eco-genetic route to warning.**
5. **Transition direction reorganizes high-trait source feasibility.**
6. **Eco-genetic closures separate into rapid-loss, persistence, and heterogeneous regimes.**
7. **Warning reliability differs between independently calibrated closures.**
8. **Discussion** — non-portability, functional loss, calibration, evolutionary rescue, and limits of generalization.
