# Manuscript workspace

**Working title:** *Directional recurrent mutation partitions trait-loss regimes in finite eco-genetic systems*

## Paper identity

This is a companion paper to `zuizui0223/eco-genetic-criticality`, not a replacement or silent revision of its closed evidence ledger. The predecessor establishes a theorem-guided finite eco-genetic framework and a conditional relative-diversity warning result under a symmetric-mutation closure. This extension asks a different question: how mutation direction changes source feasibility, realised trait-loss regimes, and whether a warning-validation domain exists at all.

The repositories remain separate. The predecessor is cited as prior framework and bridge control; its trajectories and selected domain are not evidence for this paper.

## Primary claim

At fixed mutation-map relaxation strength, changing the mutation-only equilibrium partitions the declared finite eco-genetic system into rapid-loss, persistent, and seed-heterogeneous regimes. Under the preregistered calibration family, this partition can eliminate every domain eligible for warning validation.

## Claims not made

- This paper does not show that directional mutation preserves genetic early warning.
- It does not estimate biological mutation rates.
- `no_domain_selected` is not interpreted as absence of ecological change.
- Stage III lead/lag outcomes are not claimed because Protocol 002 selected no coordinate.
- Finite-model results remain Type S evidence for the declared closure.

## Evidence completed

- Stage 0: algebraic and implementation certificate for 15 mutation coordinates.
- Stage I: 3,375 independent source-reconstruction/projection attempts.
- Stage II: 20,250 trait-loss-only calibration attempts in 810 candidate batches.
- Selection: 648 complete five-seed candidates, zero eligible candidates, and 15/15 coordinates recorded as `no_domain_selected`.
- Diagnostic audit: 322 complete candidates above the eligibility band, 242 below it, and 84 mixed across it.
