## Citation boundary

- Cite the predecessor repository or its eventual manuscript for its H1, H3, and symmetric H2-R numerical results.
- Cite the extension repository or integrated data package for Stage I–III numerical results.
- Do not use external literature to imply that the exact model result has already been demonstrated empirically.
- Do not describe `p_mu*` as an observed mutation spectrum.
- Use review papers to orient broad fields, but cite original studies for central empirical or theoretical claims when space permits.

## Metadata verification queue

Before journal submission, verify title, author order, year, journal, volume, pages/article number, and DOI for every cited item against Crossref or the publisher record. Particular care is needed for the Boettiger & Hastings papers, Govaert et al. 2019, Storz et al. 2019, and Cano et al. 2023 because the current review groups multiple closely related works.
