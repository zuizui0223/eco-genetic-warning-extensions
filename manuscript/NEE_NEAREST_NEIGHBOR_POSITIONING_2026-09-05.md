# NEE nearest-neighbour positioning — 2026-09-05

## Purpose

Clarify the flagship's novelty against the closest current Nature Ecology & Evolution neighbours without overstating priority.

## Closest model-validation neighbour

Song & Levine (2025), *Rigorous validation of ecological models against empirical time series* (Nature Ecology & Evolution 9:2265–2278; doi:10.1038/s41559-025-02864-8), introduces covariance criteria that provide necessary conditions for the validity of ecological **models** against empirical time series, including case studies on predator–prey functional responses, eco-evolutionary dynamics and higher-order interactions.

That paper and the EG flagship share a commitment to fail-closed validation, but they validate different objects.

- **Song & Levine:** Is the proposed dynamical model compatible with observable covariance relationships in empirical time series?
- **EG flagship:** Before model validation or forecast scoring, has the biological state been distinguished, has the state representation preserved target-relevant information, has the candidate warning been tested on the full event/non-event denominator, and has the empirical measurement earned the state role claimed for it?

The flagship's intended novelty is therefore **upstream predictive-object validation**, not a competing general method for validating model equations. A model can pass a model-validity test and still receive an insufficient state representation, an unvalidated early signal or a biologically plausible but endpoint-inadequate measurement.

## Closest current forecasting neighbour

Ovaskainen et al. (2026), *A digital twin for real-time biodiversity forecasting with citizen science data* (Nature Ecology & Evolution 10:481–495; doi:10.1038/s41559-025-02966-3), demonstrates a continuously updating biodiversity digital twin and validates its predictions with independent data.

This confirms that real-time ecological prediction and independent predictive validation are central current topics in the journal. The flagship addresses a different layer: **what information is entitled to enter such a forecast as state or signal in the first place?**

## Novelty statement for the Article

A concise reader-facing positioning sentence is:

> Recent work has formalized rigorous validation of ecological model equations and demonstrated scalable real-time biodiversity forecasting; our results address a logically prior problem—whether the biological state, analytical representation, warning signal and empirical measurement supplied to a forecast are themselves valid for the declared target and horizon.

## Claim ceiling

Do not claim that no previous ecological literature has discussed state definition, indicator validation, warning false positives or forecast horizons. The novelty is the **constructive integration and testing of four non-implications as one predictive-validity hierarchy**, supported by exact results, prospectively locked simulation evidence, full-denominator warning validation and natural measurement diagnostics.
