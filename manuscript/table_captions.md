# Supplementary table captions

## Table S1. Paired fragmentation effect sizes in the first-phase H1/H3 campaign

One-large and equal-isolated final-state summaries across 1,055 H1-qualified paired replicates from 12 predeclared primary cells. Interaction is the mean final `q` across patches, local effective size is the mean final patch-level effective size, and realised high-trait mass is the stored metapopulation mean. Paired reductions are descriptive finite-model effect sizes calculated from already locked outcomes; no parent simulation was rerun.

## Table S2. Model, calibration, and validation-domain contrast

Ecological parameters, recurrent-transition parameters, calibration family, deterioration schedule, seed separation, warning endpoints, and evidence boundary for the inherited symmetric benchmark, recalibrated symmetric domain, and directional calibrated domain. The table makes explicit that the two Stage III domains differ in `A_ref`, interaction-feedback `kappa`, `kappa_mu`, `p_star`, barrier increase, and horizon; Stage III is therefore a calibrated-domain portability comparison rather than a single-factor experiment.

## Table S3. Source-reconstruction support across recurrent-transition coordinates

Attempt counts and support rates for each transition coordinate and ecological parameter cell. Each planned source attempt is counted once. Projection was run only after source preparation succeeded; unsupported preparation is therefore distinct from projection failure.

## Table S4. Warning-blind common-grid calibration and event-regime classification

Candidate-level and seed-block trait-loss frequencies under strict Protocol 002. Rapid-loss, persistence, and seed-heterogeneous labels describe the distribution of seed-block loss frequencies. The original `no_domain_selected` outcome means that no candidate on the coarse common grid satisfied the strict all-seed eligibility rule; it is not a test of warning performance and does not imply structural impossibility outside that grid.

## Table S5. Fresh-seed warning endpoint summaries and secondary uncertainty audit

For each Stage III endpoint and calibrated domain: full attempted denominator, censoring categories, lead/tie/lag ordering, conventional median positive lead time and horizon-normalized lead time with whole-trajectory bootstrap intervals. The companion between-domain table reports directional-minus-symmetric median differences for absolute, horizon-normalized and hold-normalized timing, with intervals calculated by independently resampling whole trajectories within each domain.

## Table S6. Warning-blind R4 recovery and allele-frequency-connectivity condition mapping

High-rep seed-block and pooled realised functional-loss rates for the recurrent-transition frontier and Phase-E migration condition. The table records independent Phase C/D replay of R4 at `kappa_mu=0.35, p_star=0.35`, R3 neighboring coordinates, migration-level R4/R3 classifications at the fixed anchor, and paired `loss→no loss` / `no loss→loss` trajectory switches relative to isolation. Warning and diversity outcomes were unavailable during these condition analyses. `migration_rate` is allele-frequency mixing only and is not interpreted as demographic, pollinator or seed movement.

## Numbering rule

The Letter uses Figures 1–6 as its six main display items and has no main-text tables or text boxes. All tabular syntheses are supplementary and numbered Tables S1–S6. Any additional diagnostic table must be numbered from Table S7 onward and cited explicitly in the Supplement before inclusion in the submission bundle.
