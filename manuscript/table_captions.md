# Supplementary table captions

## Table S1. Model and protocol contrast

Ecological and genetic closure, calibration family, seed separation, warning endpoints, and evidence boundary for the predecessor benchmark and directional-transition extension. Rows describe declared protocols rather than pooled experiments. Parent and extension trajectories remain in separate evidence ledgers.

## Table S2. Headline evidence across the integrated study

Source reconstruction, event-regime calibration, and warning validation results used in the narrative. Counts of warning endpoints are descriptive summaries of repeated measures from the same simulated trajectories; the six endpoints within a trajectory are correlated and are not independent biological replicates.

## Table S3. Source-reconstruction support across transition coordinates

Attempt counts and support rates for each transition coordinate and ecological parameter cell. Each planned source attempt is counted once. Projection was run only after source preparation succeeded; unsupported preparation is therefore distinct from projection failure.

## Table S4. Warning-blind calibration and event-regime classification

Candidate-level and seed-block trait-loss frequencies under the preregistered calibration family. Rapid-loss-side, persistence-side, and seed-heterogeneous labels describe the distribution of seed-block loss frequencies. `no_domain_selected` means that no candidate satisfied the declared all-seed eligibility rule; it is not a test of warning performance.

## Table S5. Fresh-seed warning endpoint summaries

Availability, censoring, lead, tie, lag, and positive lead-time summaries for the symmetric bridge and directional transition. The statistical unit is the simulated trajectory. The 5%, 10%, and 20% endpoints for `H_alpha` and `H_gamma` are correlated repeated summaries within trajectories and must not be interpreted as independent replicates.

## Numbering rule

The Letter uses Figures 1–6 as its six main display items and has no main-text tables or text boxes. All tabular syntheses are supplementary and numbered Tables S1–S5. Any additional diagnostic table must be numbered from Table S6 onward and cited explicitly in `supplementary_methods.md` or a supplementary-results file before inclusion in the submission bundle.
