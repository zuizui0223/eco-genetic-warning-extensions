This document is the human-readable publication router.  Its machine-readable
counterpart is `publication_lanes.json`.

## Integrated source archive

`main_text.md` preserves the integrated pre-split narrative because historical
tests, display allocations, and submission-bundle contracts depend on it.  It
is an **integrated source archive, not an active submission manuscript**.  New
reader-facing claim development must enter exactly one of the two active lanes.

The archive may be used to trace provenance or recover shared prose, but it must
not be supplied as a third manuscript, cited as the current publication source
of truth, or used to weaken a lane-specific claim ceiling.

## Shared scientific invariants

- Parent and extension trajectories are never pooled.
- Frozen endpoints, seeds, schedules, and denominators are not retuned.
- Phase U/N/T negative results are precision-bounded nulls, not equivalence.
- Access failures, metadata STOPs, `not_identifiable`, and `not_estimable`
  outcomes remain visible.
- No lane may repair a locked source mismatch after seeing its consequence.
- `claim_evidence_map.md` and `artifact_index.md` remain shared evidence gates;
  the lane router changes ownership, not scientific results.
