## Mechanistic claim

The manuscript does **not** claim that positive alignment is universally protective. The prospective decomposition instead supports pathway competition:

- cross-layer covariance changes immediate support variance at fixed marginals;
- trait and allele organization interact strongly and non-additively;
- indirect allele-linked recruitment / interaction-dependent selection / demographic feedback can transmit spatial mismatch without direct trait or allele input to q;
- direct eco-genetic feedback into q can compensate that mismatch by strengthening weak local interaction states.

The earlier locked approximately +5 percentage-point propagation result is retained as one prospectively locked realization, not as a universal directional law.
